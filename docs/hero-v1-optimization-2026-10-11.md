## Initial findings and decisions

- The active scroll file's historical `900p` name is misleading: FFprobe measures 1920×1080, 481 frames at 24 fps, 20.042 seconds, 161 I frames and 320 P frames. The other `scroll-v2` file has the same metadata and size (42,949,826 bytes).
- V1 currently preloads three videos and serializes seeks with a 33 ms throttle. The ambient loop and its fog/firefly animation are unwanted and will be removed only from V1.
- A temporary all-I-frame CRF 10 experiment preserved dimensions/count but measured SSIM 0.996207 and grew to 128 MiB. It is not pixel-identical and is not an approved release asset. The original video is retained while scheduling is improved first.
- The About paper wrapper animates both full two-chapter opacity and position over a live fixed shader. Its tiled SVG turbulence is an additional paint cost.
- Bespoke `h1`, Hospitality timeline `h2` and stage copy explicitly use `overflow-y:auto`; these are the source of the unwanted internal bars, not the authored horizontal page progression.
- All seven current gallery-index second photographs are native 3200px on their long axis. The portrait cards' width-only `sizes` declarations underestimate the pixels needed for landscape images cropped to tall frames and then scaled.

## Verification ledger

Iteration 1 removed the ambient decoder/fireflies and the 33 ms seek throttle. An actual browser handoff/settle measured three native seeks averaging 209.3 ms (max 213.7 ms), so this was insufficient. Iteration 2 adds a progressive WebCodecs decoder and native canvas rendering of the original AVC samples. The source MP4 is unchanged; its reproducible byte-position/configuration index is generated by `scripts/prepare-hero-video-index.mjs`. A twelve-frame cache bounds decoded memory, all frames remain 1920×1080, and the native player remains the compatibility/replacement-media fallback. Source hash and packet-byte identity, cache bounds, disposal and fallback have focused tests. [WebCodecs VideoDecoder reference](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/VideoDecoder) and [frame presentation observation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/HTMLVideoElement/requestVideoFrameCallback) informed the implementation.

The V1 page now edits the same focused `home.variants` item as the published-home collection. Historical scalar fields seed that collection when it has not been saved; a second scalar editor no longer competes with it. The homepage selector remains Home > Published homepage > Choose the live opening.

Iteration 3 shortened decode batches, isolated cold loading from gesture timing, preferred the software AVC decoder, and cancelled obsolete jobs after large direction changes. Five browser gestures exposed a remaining hold: exact-target-only drawing could miss a continuously changing target. Iteration 4 uses a directional nine-frame lookahead and a bounded 24-frame cache, presents the nearest available original frame during motion, and settles on the exact target. No synthetic, interpolated or re-encoded frames are used.

Iteration 4 showed cached forward updates at 1.9–12.4 ms, but its reverse lookahead decoded predecessors before the requested target. Iteration 5 separates these jobs: decode the target's short GOP first, then refill the directional buffer; interrupt obsolete prefetch sooner when a missing target moves beyond six frames. The regression test verifies that an uncached reverse target reaches the decoder before its predecessor buffer. [Superseded iteration 3 measurements](hero-v1-evidence/iteration-3-scroll.json) and [iteration 4 measurements](hero-v1-evidence/iteration-4-scroll.json) preserve the unfavorable observations rather than presenting them as final acceptance.

Iteration 6 expands directional prefetch to 21/45 frames with conservative/desktop cache caps of 48/96 native frames, lets the browser choose hardware or software decoding, and softens Lenis easing from .24 to .12. The [final five-case browser record](hero-v1-evidence/final-scroll.json) shows exact settled targets at 10.7083, 14.0833, 9.0417, 8.7917 and 20 seconds. Cached small adjustments measured 2.7–2.8 ms; the fast-forward/reverse endpoints measured 30.9/49.2 ms. The final large jump measured 152.2 ms; the run's mean app presentation latency was 33.2 ms, its maximum 183.3 ms, and cold loading was 295.7 ms. These are application measurements, not browser FPS: large uncached jumps still take longer than cached motion. The original video is byte-identical. Memory-limited/unknown devices use the smaller cache, and every retained frame is closed on disposal.

Scoped production-build browser checks: Bespoke's opening heading fits the initial frame with visible overflow and no nested scroller. Hospitality's heading and all seven stage copy boxes have visible overflow, retain their full text and fit without nested bars; the horizontal timeline remains intentional. About's white two-chapter wrapper has opacity 1 and no transform; founders and Why OKNO retain their paper treatment. All four requested project preview links reach their original detail routes. Landscape previews now request 1920px/q90 instead of the undersized 640px/q75 requests; portrait previews retain appropriately smaller responsive requests.

The full suite passed 80 files/309 tests with two workers before the final decoder tuning; the final scoped decoder/native-fallback/intro suite passed nine checks. The subsequent storage recovery suite passed 81 files/314 tests, lint and production build/type checking. All 30 release function traces passed, with the largest 179.7 MiB and private files excluded. Quantitative browser FPS/CWV and physical-device acceptance are separate from source metadata, build checks and observed behavior.
