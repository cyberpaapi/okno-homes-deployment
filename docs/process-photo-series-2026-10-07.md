Five newly generated, photorealistic **concept visualizations** replace the earlier stills in the detailed Process timeline. They are not photographs of a documented OKNO build. The existing Process film and its source frames are unchanged. All previous timeline image files remain in `public/process/` and can be restored by changing the five default `image` paths in `ProcessDetailTimeline.tsx`.

## Visual continuity and production

- Built-in `image_gen` was used for five final stills. Its underlying model name and per-image cost were not surfaced by the tool. There is no FPS for still images.
- Generated PNGs are 1672 × 941. Final images were encoded as WebP at quality 86 without resizing; the five files together are about 1.9 MB.
- The original animated gabled house and its tropical site supplied the architectural silhouette and sequence. `public/assets/projects/real-v1/kray-sangareddy-01.webp` supplied references for plausible timber, cladding, glass and photographic texture; the Kray roof shape was not used.
- The series uses one compact gabled home: dark standing-seam roof with two slim rooflights, charcoal sides, warm timber glazed gable, and modest raised foundation. The land/site images share one coastal clearing. All frames use restrained natural late-afternoon light and real-world scale, with no former cyan guide lights.

## Prompt set

### 1. Brief and site

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: FIRST of five 16:9 editorial photographs for a modular-home process timeline, BEFORE any construction. Generate a new real-looking photograph. The reference image shows the same tropical coastal site and the future home's design; use its vegetation, shoreline, earth tones, and quiet late-afternoon photographic treatment for continuity, but REMOVE THE HOUSE COMPLETELY. A modest cleared building pad set safely back from the water, a narrow site path, two small survey tripods/total-station markers and one architect/surveyor in muted work clothes at human scale reading the land. Do not show footings, building, construction machinery or a finished structure yet. Camera: modest elevated oblique view, wide 16:9 landscape, grounded geographic scale, believable site access and topography. Soft overcast-gold after-rain daylight, authentic greens and timber-earth palette, subtle film grain, realistic textures and shadows. No cyan glowing markers, no wireframe, no map graphics, no labels, no logo, no fantasy vegetation, no impossible perspective, no AI-painterly look. Keep central site legible with ample real landscape context.

### 2. Design and approvals

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: SECOND of five 16:9 editorial photographs for the same modular-home Process timeline, design and approvals stage. Generate a NEW natural documentary photograph that belongs with reference image 1's late-afternoon coastal light, forest greens, charcoal and warm timber. Reference image 1 establishes the actual house design only: compact rectangular single-storey cabin, simple pitched dark metal roof with two slim rooflights, charcoal side walls, timber-framed glazed gable. Reference image 2 establishes the site and photographic grade. Scene: real architect's worktable under a simple shaded studio veranda facing lush tropical vegetation, large unbranded architectural floor plan and section drawings for that specific compact gabled home, a small physical timber/card model accurately showing its pitched roof and glazed gable, restrained samples of charcoal metal siding and warm timber, pencils and tracing sheets. Most of the frame is tactile documentation and model; no full-size house on the site yet. Camera: candid editorial close three-quarter view, 35mm lens, selective but sufficient focus on the model and layout, authentic paper edges and imperfections, wide 16:9 horizontal composition. Soft daylight after rain, restrained green/charcoal/timber palette, true photographic textures. Keep drawings diagrammatic with no readable fake text or pseudo labels. No computer UI, no cyan glow, no graphic overlays, no fantasy architecture, no logo, no watermark, no AI gloss.

### 3. Factory manufacturing

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: THIRD of five 16:9 editorial Process timeline photographs, factory manufacturing stage. Generate a NEW documentary-style photograph of real modular wall and floor panels being fabricated in a controlled workshop. Reference image 1 defines the exact future home design: compact gabled cabin with charcoal cladding, warm timber framing and glazed gable. Reference image 2 defines realistic timber/metal/glazing materials in an OKNO built project; it is not the same roof shape. Show a credible factory BEFORE house assembly: several large timber-frame wall panels laid on horizontal benches, one gable-end frame with sized window/glazing opening, insulation and services being fitted, black cladding samples nearby, precision tools and organized equipment. One or two workers at normal scale in practical safety clothing, viewed from behind or side, engaged in genuine panel work. No assembled miniature house and no complete home on a platform. Camera: professional architectural documentary photography, 35mm, wide horizontal composition with foreground panel details and manufacturing depth; straight lines and believable joints, natural imperfect surfaces. Lighting: balanced soft daylight from high workshop windows plus neutral industrial lights; rich but legible charcoal, warm wood and muted green-gray, visually coherent with the reference site's restrained editorial grade. Avoid cyan LEDs, blue glowing edges, science-fiction machines, sterile CGI, labels, logos, text, watermark, implausible structure or extra limbs.
